The Problem

As we discussed on our call, [Client First Name], [Client Business Name] has a lot going for it. You have a proven service, happy clients, and a real impact on the people you work with. The foundation is there.

But there are a few things holding the business back from scaling to where you want it to be.

01

Selling a service, not an outcome.

Right now, [Client Business Name] is priced per session or per package. So is every other [niche] in [city]. When the market sells the same way, the only thing left to compete on is price. The businesses making real money in this space sell the outcome instead. When you sell the outcome, you have more margin left over. This means you can scale faster by increasing your marketing spend. You also have more money left over to hire talent etc.

02

No real sales process.

Leads come in, but there's no clear, structured path moving them from initial interest to a confident "yes." Without that path in place, even strong prospects lose momentum and go quiet.

03

Lead flow is unpredictable.

Referrals and word-of-mouth are valuable, but they're inconsistent. To scale predictably, you need a lead source you can switch on, scale up, and rely on month to month.

04

Paid ads tend to bring the wrong leads.

Most owners who try Meta ads end up with leads who can't afford the work. The leads aren't the problem. The system around them is.

05

Growth is capped by your time.

Every deal still runs through you. That means the business can only grow as fast as you personally can. Until that bottleneck is removed, scaling is going to feel like working harder rather than smarter.

"These aren't separate issues. They're connected, and they compound each other. You can't fix the lead flow without fixing the offer. That's why most agencies fail their clients. We solve all of them together."

The Solution

I build done-for-you client acquisition systems. I design the offer, run the ads, qualify the leads, and close the deals.

Here's how it works.

01

Offer Creation

This is the foundation. Before we spend a penny on ads, I build your offer with you. We repackage your service around the transformation your client actually wants. This alone changes the kind of buyer you attract.

02

Tracking & Tech Foundation

A properly configured ad account, integrated calendar, and automated lead notifications. I set everything up so it runs cleanly from day one.

03

Ad Creation & Campaign Launch

I write the scripts, edit the footage for Meta, build the landing pages, and launch. I then manage the campaigns daily, scaling what works.

04

Lead Working & Qualification

The system contacts every new lead within minutes. Leads that are a fit get booked onto the sales calendar, reminded, and sent prep materials.

05

Sales Call Closing

I personally take every qualified sales call. You don't get involved until the new client has signed and paid.

The result is a steady, predictable pipeline of clients buying an offer you love delivering, without you lifting a finger on the acquisition side.
